OUR STORY

Created for connection.

PANA is a contemporary dining and entertainment destination in Fourways, Johannesburg.

The concept brings together food, drinks, hospitality and entertainment to create an environment where people can connect and enjoy memorable social experiences.

PANA is designed for friends, couples, professionals, social diners and people looking for a destination where dining and entertainment come together.
